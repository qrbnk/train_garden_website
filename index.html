How it works · for companies

Bidding on a tender with social value requirements? We'll help you draft the proposal, or track delivery once you've won, with real, verified evidence behind every figure.

01

Paste what you committed to

Paste the social value commitments from the awarded tender, exactly as written.

02

We propose a fulfilment plan

Concrete solutions and realistic timeframes for each criterion you committed to.

03

You review and accept

Nothing gets tracked until you accept the plan.

04

Evidence builds automatically

As delivery happens, real evidence (documents, certificates, activity logs) attaches to each criterion. A verified case, not a self-report.

05

Export a report anytime

Every figure in the report expands to show exactly what evidence sits behind it.

Questions

Quick answers

Who is Train Garden for?expand_more

Community members and organisations, local authorities, property developers and contractors, and social value professionals. One platform connects all four around the local area.

Is it free to use?expand_more

Always for community users. Built environment companies subscribe, and local authorities are priced against the programme's existing grant or department budget.

What is the difference between Tier 1 and Tier 2?expand_more

Tier 1 covers post-tender compliance only. Tier 2 is pre- and post-tender, end-to-end. There is also a hiring fee, discounted for subscribers.

How is the evidence verified?expand_more

As delivery happens, real evidence such as documents, certificates and activity logs attaches to each criterion. Every figure in the report expands to show what sits behind it, so it is a verified case rather than a self-report.

How are local authorities priced?expand_more

Priced against the programme's existing grant or department budget, negotiated per programme, and not sold as generic SaaS.

Can it be used beyond urban regeneration?expand_more

Yes. The platform tools apply to other projects too, from energy projects to community engagement for the charity sector.

How do I get started?expand_more

Email info@traingarden.co.uk or book a 30-minute call and tell us what you're working on. We'll shape the right package with you.
